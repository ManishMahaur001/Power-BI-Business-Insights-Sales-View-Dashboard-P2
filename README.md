# 📊 Business Insights 360 – Sales View

## 📌 Overview

The **Sales View** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customers, products, and market performance.

It helps business owners and decision-makers understand **what is driving sales, where profitability is improving or declining, and which areas require business attention.**

## 🎯 Objectives

* Monitor overall sales performance
* Analyze revenue and profitability
* Identify high-performing products
* Analyze customer contribution
* Compare markets and regions
* Understand cost and margin structure
* Identify business growth opportunities

## 📈 Key Analysis

### 💰 Sales Performance

* Net Sales analysis
* Sales trends and performance comparison
* P&L breakdown
* Sales performance across different periods

### 📦 Product Analysis

* Product-wise sales performance
* Product-wise Gross Margin
* Identification of high and low-performing products
* Comparison of product contribution to overall sales

### 👥 Customer Analysis

* Customer-wise revenue analysis
* Identification of high-value customers
* Customer contribution to sales and profitability
* Comparison of customers based on revenue and margin

### 🌍 Market Analysis

* Market-wise sales performance
* Region-wise performance
* Comparison of revenue and Gross Margin across markets
* Identification of strong and weak markets

## 🔍 Business Results

The dashboard can generate the following important business results:

* **Sales Drivers:** Identify which products, customers, and markets contribute most to revenue.
* **Profitability Drivers:** Identify areas generating strong Gross Margin and areas where margins are under pressure.
* **Product Opportunities:** Find products with high sales but comparatively lower margins that may require pricing or cost optimization.
* **Customer Opportunities:** Identify high-revenue customers and evaluate whether they are also generating healthy margins.
* **Market Opportunities:** Compare regions and markets to identify areas with strong growth potential.
* **Cost Impact:** Analyze the relationship between sales deductions, COGS, and Gross Margin to understand where profitability is being lost.

## 💡 Recommendations 

Based on the analysis available in this Sales View, a business owner can take the following actions:

### 1. Focus on High-Margin Products

Prioritize products that generate both **strong sales and high Gross Margin**. Increase their availability, marketing, and promotional focus.

### 2. Review Low-Margin Products

Products generating high revenue but low margins should be reviewed for:

* Pricing changes
* Supplier cost reduction
* Discount optimization
* Better inventory management

### 3. Strengthen High-Value Customers

Identify customers contributing significantly to revenue and build strategies such as:

* Loyalty programs
* Personalized offers
* Cross-selling
* Upselling
* Retention campaigns

### 4. Improve Weak Markets

Markets with lower sales or profitability should be investigated before increasing investment. Analyze:

* Customer demand
* Pricing
* Competition
* Distribution
* Marketing effectiveness

### 5. Reduce Unnecessary Costs

Use the P&L and COGS analysis to identify areas where costs are reducing profitability. Negotiating supplier prices and improving operational efficiency can directly improve margins.

### 6. Optimize Discounts

Avoid excessive discounts on products that already have strong demand. Instead, use targeted discounts to improve sales of slow-moving or low-performing products.

### 7. Allocate Marketing Budget Better

Marketing investment should be concentrated on **high-potential products, customers, and markets** rather than distributing the budget equally across all segments.

### 8. Monitor KPIs Regularly

Business owners should regularly track:

* Net Sales
* Gross Margin %
* Net Profit %
* Product performance
* Customer contribution
* Market performance

This allows management to identify problems early and make faster, data-driven decisions.
